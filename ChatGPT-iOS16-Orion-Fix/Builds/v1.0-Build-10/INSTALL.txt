ChatGPT iOS 16 Orion Fix — Version 1.0 Build 10

1. Disable Builds 1 through 9 in Orion.
2. Install the Build 10 ZIP itself; do not extract it first.
3. Enable only Build 10.
4. Open the extension popup and turn Light Diagnostics (Build 10) ON.
5. Reload https://chatgpt.com once. Do not clear cookies or cache.
6. Try the sidebar and sending a harmless test message.
7. Wait about 10 seconds.
8. Open the extension popup, tap Refresh Report, then Copy Report.
9. Paste the saved report into ChatGPT from another working device if needed.

Diagnostics default OFF. When OFF, the early error listeners are detached, the transient early error/feature buffers are cleared, and no diagnostic report is saved.
